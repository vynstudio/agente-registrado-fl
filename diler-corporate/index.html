In plain words

What is a registered agent?

Florida requires every LLC and corporation to name a registered agent: a person or business with a Florida street address that accepts legal papers and official state mail for the company during business hours.

When you form with us, Diler Services, LLC is named as your registered agent. We receive what arrives for your company and send it on to you, so you don't miss a deadline, wherever you live.

Included for the first year with every package, then $119 per year.

Questions

Before you start.

Do I need to live in Florida or the US?

No. Anyone can own a Florida LLC or corporation. You need a registered agent with a Florida address, which we provide.

How long does formation take?

Most filings are submitted within 1 business day and approved by the state in 1–5 business days. Your EIN usually follows within a few days.

LLC or corporation: which is right for me?

Many small businesses choose an LLC for its simplicity; a corporation can suit plans to raise investment. We give general information only. A CPA or attorney can advise on your situation, and we can refer you to one.

What happens after the first year?

Registered agent service renews at $119 per year. We can also file your Florida annual report for $119 plus the state fee.

Are you a law firm?

No. We prepare and file documents and act as registered agent. We don't give legal or tax advice.
